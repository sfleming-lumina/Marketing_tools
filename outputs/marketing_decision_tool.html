Usage guide

How to use this workspace

Use one repeatable workflow: discover what needs attention, investigate the evidence, test a potential decision, and create an implementation brief.

Discover → Investigate → Test → Implement

  • Discover: choose an insight from the Command center priority queue.
  • Investigate: the active question and filters follow you into Funnel lab or Geo intelligence; add the evidence you want carried forward.
  • Test: Scenario studio opens with a relevant starting preset while preserving the same campaign, county / market, region, and period.
  • Implement: choose Track automatically. The tool captures the authenticated owner, decision, scope, baseline, assumptions, evidence, expected impact, and review window without forms or uploads.

Decision tracker

Open Decisions from the sidebar to see every tracked choice. Current lakehouse cohorts are compared with each frozen baseline automatically. Spend changes are labeled as observed implementation signals; conversion improvements remain outcome signals unless an external execution system confirms the action.

Filters and operating regions

Campaign lists campaigns with cohort activity in the selected period and operating region. Campaign and county / market selections re-query the lakehouse so the funnel, trends, CAC, geography, scenarios, and assistant context all use the same exact slice. County grain deliberately recombines lead-attribution rows with their downstream AHJs; resolved AHJ examples remain available in each cohort for investigation. Last 30 days uses the current weekly cohort plus four prior weekly cohort starts because the governed runtime is weekly/monthly, not daily. Reset restores the seven-month operating-footprint view; Refresh forces fresh funnel, geography, campaign-catalog, and reconciliation requests.

Command center

The opportunity queue ranks campaign × county decisions by estimated win impact, confidence, actionability, and the active time window. The decision canvas plots CAC against lead-to-win conversion; bubble size represents lead volume and color represents the recommended decision type. Campaign panels add benchmark and confidence context. Hover any opportunity to highlight it everywhere, or click it to investigate the exact pair.

Funnel lab

  • Green marks improving or efficient performance, amber indicates watch conditions, and red identifies intervention areas.
  • Auto switches to lead-first when spend coverage is incomplete.
  • Spend shows attributable spend even when coverage warrants caution.
  • Lead cohort follows leads from creation through later outcomes.
  • Trend reference lines show the selected cohort average; the shaded latest-period range and inflection marker focus attention without hiding the observed values.
  • The waterfall always preserves the complete stage sequence. Positive bars are strengths to protect, negative bars are recoverable gaps, and the callout beneath the chart names the highest-value action. Neutral bars mean the slice needs more mature benchmark evidence.
  • CAC means effective attributed spend ÷ cohort wins and is qualified by the coverage badge.
  • Aged fallout separates leads open without a set after 30 days, sets without a run after 30 days, and runs without a win after 60 days.

Geo intelligence

Rank counties and AHJs using cohort conversion, benchmark delta, active pipeline, sample strength, and spend coverage. Use the operating-region filter before comparing markets.

Scenario studio

Adjust assumptions to explore directional outcomes. Scenarios are planning aids, not forecasts; the baseline always comes from the selected live cohort.
